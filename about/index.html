Yauhen Lazurkin

Software products from Lübeck

I’m Yauhen Lazurkin, an independent software developer based in Lübeck, Germany. My main work is designing, building and running small, focused software products. I care about simplicity: a product should do one thing well and get out of the way.

Products

  • Text2Event: create calendar events from a plain-text description.
  • GrammarMama: an AI-powered adaptive language tutor with personalized daily exercises.
  • TV Radar: subscribe to TV series and get notified when a new episode drops.
  • Talklist: shopping lists you say out loud, turned into a tidy, shareable checklist.

More on the Projects page, and I write about what I learn along the way on the Blog.

Background

Before building my own products I spent 13+ years in software engineering, mostly Java and Go backends, distributed systems and CI/CD, in FinTech, transportation and AdTech, including several years leading engineering teams. My master’s-equivalent degree in Systems Engineering and Artificial Intelligence is from BSUIR (evaluated by German ANABIN).

For consulting and contract work, see my CV.
