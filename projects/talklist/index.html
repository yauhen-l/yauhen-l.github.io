Talklist

October 21, 2026

Talklist turns what you say into a tidy, shareable shopping checklist. Tap the mic, say “milk, two avocados, bread and pasta”, and the app splits it into items for you. No account, no ads, no tracking cookies.

Key features:

  • Just say it — dictate your list in one sentence, or type it
  • Sorted for you — items are grouped by category, quantities are picked out of what you say, and duplicates are merged
  • Shop together — share a link and everyone sees ticks within about half a minute, no account needed
  • Works offline — keep ticking items off without signal; changes sync when you are back online
  • Multilingual — English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Russian, including product names from other languages
